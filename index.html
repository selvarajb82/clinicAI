Billing & Coverage FAQ

What should I bring to verify my insurance coverage?

Please bring your current photo ID, physical insurance card, and any co-pay amount required by your policy. Our check-in team will scan and verify benefits upon your arrival.

What if the clinic is out-of-network for my insurance provider?

If we are out-of-network, we can still file claims on your behalf as a courtesy. However, you may be responsible for a higher co-insurance percentage. We recommend checking out-of-network benefits with your insurer prior to scheduling.

Are there self-pay discounts for uninsured patients?

Yes! We offer simplified self-pay rates. A standard medical consultation begins at $95. Standard laboratory panels and childhood immunizations are priced competitively. Please contact our front desk for a comprehensive self-pay price list.
